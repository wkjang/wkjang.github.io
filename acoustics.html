Infant Cry Acoustics

Infant cries are structured signals. I segment cries from onset, extract their temporal envelope and spectral structure, and compare acoustic profiles across ages and cry types to describe how crying becomes organized over early development.

Research question
How does the acoustic structure of crying become organized over early development?
Data
Open infant-cry audio corpus
Unit of analysis
Cry vocalization
Methods
Signal processing, spectral features, clustering
